## Getting started

**How do I log a meal?**  
Tap "+" on the main tab. You get five entry methods: photo scan (point your camera at the plate), barcode scanner (works with packaged foods worldwide via Open Food Facts), Quick Database (search our 3074-item catalog), voice ("I had tomato soup and a sandwich"), or recipe (build from your saved book).

**The photo scan can't recognise my dish.**  
Try a clearer angle with the whole plate in frame. If it still mis-identifies, tap any detected item to edit name + grams. Our model improves with every correction.

**My calorie totals are off by ~10-15%.**  
Open Profile → AI Calibration. The app learns your portion sizes over time — you can also manually nudge the calibration factor.

## Apple Health

Profile → Integrations → "Import weight from Apple Health". We only read **Body Mass** (your weight). Nothing else, ever. Permission is requested only when you tap the button.

## Account

**Delete my account?**  
Profile → Settings → "Delete account". Wipes all server data within 30 days and the local SwiftData immediately. Irreversible.

**Export my data?**  
Profile → Settings → "Download JSON / CSV / Full ZIP" — get everything in machine-readable form.

**Sign in problems?**  
We support Sign in with Apple, Google, and Email magic link. If a magic link doesn't arrive within 2 minutes, check spam. Google requires a popup; on some EU corporate networks it gets blocked.

## Troubleshooting

**App crashes on launch.**  
Force-quit, reopen. If it persists, reinstall (your cloud data survives — just sign in again).

**Notifications not arriving?**  
Settings → Notifications → Mealgram → make sure Alerts/Sounds/Banners are on. Inside the app: Profile → Settings → Reminders → toggle each channel.

**Watch app shows nothing?**  
Open Mealgram on the iPhone first, log one meal, then the Watch face will sync within a few seconds.
