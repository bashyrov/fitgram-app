## Description (4000 chars)

```
Mealgram is a Polish-market calorie tracker built for people who are tired of nagging diet apps. Instead of micro-counting every kcal, you get a calm companion that knows Polish flavours and respects your rhythm.

▸ FIVE WAYS TO LOG A MEAL

📸 Photo — snap a plate, AI identifies ingredients and estimates portions
📦 Barcode — scan products from Polish stores (Lidl, Biedronka, Carrefour, Auchan)
🔎 Quick database — 160+ Polish dishes and products, from schabowy to sernik
🎙 Voice — say "chicken breast 200g, rice 80g" and you're done
📖 Recipes — your own recipe library with one-tap "Cook" button

▸ AI COACH OLA — NO JUDGEMENT, JUST RHYTHM

Ola watches your week and delivers personalized weekly summaries. No guilt-trips, no stress — just practical nudges:
• "Your protein dropped on Wednesday and Friday — add cottage cheese to lunch"
• "Great week! 5 out of 7 days within calorie goal"
• "Lunch time — what's the plan?"

▸ WEIGHT TRACKING WITH TREND

Log weight whenever. The chart shows 30-day trend, 7-day moving average, kg/week rate of change, and BMI with WHO bands. Optional sync with Apple Health.

▸ POLISH CULINARY TRADITIONS

The app knows Polish holidays and dishes:
• Wigilia, Easter, Tłusty Czwartek with food context
• Pączki, mazurek, żurek, bigos in database with values
• Polish seed: 160+ items from USDA + Open Food Facts

▸ STREAKS AND BADGES

Daily logging builds a streak. Afternoon "freeze" button protects against a one-day slip. 20+ achievement badges. Share your record as a 1080×1920 card.

▸ FRIENDS (EXPANDING)

Add friends via QR or link. See their streaks and achievements. Weekly leaderboard among friends. Fully private — only accepted people see your data.

▸ PRIVACY-FIRST

• Everything on your phone first (SwiftData)
• Photos never leave your phone except through AI proxy
• JSON / CSV / ZIP export anytime
• Full control: Profile → Delete account = gone in 30 days
• GDPR compliant

▸ WIDGET AND SIRI

Home screen widget shows streak, remaining calories, last meal. Siri shortcuts: "Hey Siri, log a meal in Mealgram". Spotlight indexes your recipes.

▸ ACCESSIBILITY

VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, reduce motion — fully supported.

▸ PREMIUM SUBSCRIPTION

7-day free trial. After: 29 PLN/month or 199 PLN/year (43% savings). Cancel anytime in iPhone Settings.

▸ CONTACT

Questions? hello@mealgram.pl
Privacy policy: mealgram.pl/privacy
Terms: mealgram.pl/terms

Mealgram — because dieting isn't a prison, it's a rhythm.
```
