Food logging

Five ways to add a meal. One clean confirmation flow.

Every route ends on the same portion screen: general mode for the whole dish, detailed mode for ingredients, and fast AI refresh when the name or product data needs a correction.

01

Photo scan

Take a picture, get a meal estimate, adjust grams, then save only when it looks right.

02

Voice input

Say what you ate. Fitgram parses products and weights, then asks you to confirm.

03

Barcode

Scan packaged food and adjust the amount. No AI needed for barcode products.

04

Manual meals

Add your own dish, use general or detailed mode, and save it to your kitchen.

05

Food base

Use the built-in library and recent meals when you already know what you ate.

06

Ola Kitchen

Pick a calorie target and get meal ideas with portions, products and recipe details.

Coach Ola

Advice that knows the situation.

Ola is separated from facts. She gives practical guidance for your current goal, streak, protein gap, evening calories, activity and progress trend.

  • Daily context-aware advice for losing, maintaining or gaining weight.
  • Helpful and not helpful reactions, so advice can become calmer over time.
  • Facts of the day stay in their own library, separate from coaching.
