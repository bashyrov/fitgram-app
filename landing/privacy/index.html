Privacy Policy

Fitgram has no ads, does not sell personal data, and keeps processing transparent. Your core data stays under your control.

Last updated: July 26, 2026Version 1.1

Short version

Your meal history, goals, photos, weight and water logs are stored on your iPhone and may sync through your private iCloud. Online features such as AI analysis, barcode lookup, account sign-in and subscriptions use trusted service providers only when needed.

1. Who we are

Fitgram is an independent iOS app published by the Fitgram team. Contact: onefitgram@gmail.com.

For GDPR requests and privacy questions, contact us at onefitgram@gmail.com.

2. What data we handle

Data you enter

  • Profile: name, email, height, weight, date of birth, sex, activity level and goals.
  • Meals: photos, dish names, nutrition values, ratings, notes and tags.
  • Activity: workouts, weight entries, water intake, recipes, achievements and streaks.

System permissions

  • Camera and photo library: to scan meals. Photos are used only for the selected feature.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: to add meals by voice when you grant permission.
  • Apple Health: optional weight/activity integration when enabled by you.
  • Notifications: local reminders, streak alerts and achievement messages.

3. Where your data lives

Fitgram uses SwiftData, Apple's local database, to store core app data on your device.

If you enable iCloud Drive, your data may sync through a private Apple CloudKit container between your devices. Apple encrypts this data and Fitgram does not sell it.

Optional online features can process limited data through service providers: Cloudflare Worker for AI proxying, Google Gemini for meal recognition, Open Food Facts for barcode lookup, Apple StoreKit for subscriptions and sign-in providers when you authenticate.

4. What we do not do

  • No advertising network tracking.
  • No selling personal data.
  • No IDFA or Apple Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No location access request.
  • No retaining meal photos after AI processing unless the app feature stores a local copy on your device.

5. Photo and AI analysis

When you use online AI features, the selected photo or meal text can be temporarily sent through our proxy to an AI provider so Fitgram can estimate food, portions and nutrition. The result is returned to the app for your review before saving.

Fitgram is not a medical device. Nutrition estimates can be inaccurate and should be reviewed by the user.

6. Sign in and payments

Fitgram supports Sign in with Apple and may support other sign-in providers. Premium subscriptions are handled by Apple StoreKit. Fitgram does not receive your card number, billing address or full payment details.

7. Your rights

You can exercise most rights directly in the app: export data, edit profile fields, delete account data and revoke permissions in iOS Settings. You can also contact us at onefitgram@gmail.com.

8. Children

Fitgram is intended for users aged 13+. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

9. Changes and contact

When this policy is updated, the new date appears above. Material changes may also be communicated inside the app.

Questions, suggestions or GDPR requests: onefitgram@gmail.com.

Polish supervisory authority: President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland.
